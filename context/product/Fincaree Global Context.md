## Hero Statement

**"A smarter workspace for advisors to build better financial relationships."**

---

## 01 / OVERVIEW

### Problem
Fragmented tools make financial advisors spend too much time managing workflows instead of advising clients.

### Solution
One connected workspace for client planning, AI-assisted workflows, follow-ups, content, and engagement.

### Impact
Less administrative effort, better client engagement, and more consistent advisory relationships.

### SWOT Analysis — a collective synthesis of research

[Image: SWOT analysis diagram with four quadrants]

**✓ STRENGTHS**
- Established advisory practices and industry standards
- Mature planning and portfolio management tools
- Existing technology ecosystem with proven capabilities
- Strong advisor expertise and client trust

**↯ WEAKNESSES**
- Fragmented workflows across multiple tools
- Repetitive administrative and manual tasks
- Manual data movement between systems
- Follow-up and record-keeping burden

**◎ OPPORTUNITIES**
- AI-assisted and automated advisor workflows
- Automated meeting preparation and follow-ups
- Integrated client education through learning paths
- Engagement intelligence for better client relationships
- Unified platform to streamline end-to-end advisory process

**! THREATS / CONSTRAINTS**
- Advisor adoption resistance and change management
- Compliance and regulatory constraints
- Integration complexity with existing systems
- Strong presence of established competitor platforms
- Trust and accuracy concerns around AI outputs

### Core Features of Fincaree

> "Turning advisor needs and opportunities into meaningful product capabilities."

---

**Feature 01 — From leads to advisory, not just lead management**

Connect marketplace leads with financial assessment, plan selection and conversion into an advisory relationship.

Flow: Marketplace Leads → Financial Assessment → Plan Selection → Advisory Client

*One seamless journey from interest to advisory.*

---

**Feature 02 — From client data to goal-led advice**

Make goals the common thread across planning, meetings, progress and reviews.

Flow: Client Profile → Planning → Meetings → Progress → Reviews → **Goals** (at center)

*Goals at the center, everything connects.*

---

**Feature 03 — From meeting scheduling to meeting readiness**

Bring context, goals, previous interactions and preparation into one workflow.

**Meeting Workspace inputs:**
- Purpose
- Goals
- Previous Interactions
- Documents
- Preparation

**Meeting Workspace outputs:**
- AI Suggested Agenda
- Talking Points
- Next Steps

*Not just schedule. Prepare. Meet. Follow up.*

---

**Feature 04 — From financial plans to actionable advice**

Connect financial analysis to actionable plans, reports and follow-ups.

Flow: Analysis → Plan & Recs → Report → Next Actions

*A continuous loop from insight to action.*

---

**Feature 05 — From content delivery to client engagement**

Use learning paths and simulations to help clients understand and participate.

**Learning Path:**
- Basics of Investing
- Risk & Return
- Goal Planning
- Insurance Essentials

**Client Progress:**
- 0% — Simulations Completed

*Educate. Engage. Empower.*

---

**Feature 06 — From tracking data to surfacing what matters**

Turn client, goal and engagement data into clear priorities and opportunities.

**Needs Attention examples:**
- AR — Retirement goal off track — High
- PP — Insurance review due — Medium
- SM — Annual review overdue — High

**Summary indicators:**
- Opportunities: +3
- Engagement Score: 0%

*Less noise, more next actions.*

## Key Terminology Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this project |
|---|---|
| **Fincaree** | The B2B SaaS product — an intelligent advisor operating layer |
| **Finny AI** | The AI assistant embedded across the advisor workflow |
| **Individual Practicing Advisor** | Primary target user — independently manages advisory + wealth + operations |
| **RIA** | Registered Investment Advisor — regulatory context for Indian/US markets |
| **Meeting Workspace** | Structured space combining context, goals, prep, AI agenda, talking points, next steps |
| **Learning Path** | Curated educational sequence delivered to clients through the platform |
| **Advisory Client** | A converted client with an active advisory relationship (vs. a Lead) |
| **Goal Gap** | A detected shortfall between a client's financial goal and their current plan |
| **Action Plan** | A structured set of next steps tied to a client goal or review |
| **Engagement Score** | A metric surfacing client participation across content, simulations, and follow-ups |
| **Growth Signals** | Indicators of client lifecycle events or wealth events worth acting on |
| **Wealth Conversion Opportunity** | A signal that a client is ready to move from advisory to wealth management |
| **WPS** | Wage Protection System — payroll compliance framework (Edenred/UAE context, not Fincaree) |
